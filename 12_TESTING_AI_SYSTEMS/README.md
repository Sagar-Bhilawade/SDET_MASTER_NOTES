# 12_TESTING_AI_SYSTEMS

How to **test products that have AI inside** (LLM chatbots, RAG assistants, summarisers, agents).
Different from `11_AI_IN_TESTING`, which is about **using AI to do QA**.

Honest status: this section is *studied knowledge*, not production experience. Every note says
what transfers from my real work (API testing, CI gates, JMeter, SQL) and never claims DeepEval,
Ragas or red-team tooling as hands-on.

Read in order — 01 to 03 are the foundation, 04 to 06 the core of "how do you test it", 07 to 09 the
risk layers, 10 to 12 delivery and interview use.
