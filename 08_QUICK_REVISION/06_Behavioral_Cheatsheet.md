# Behavioral Cheat Sheet — HR / Behavioral Round

> Dense recall for `07_INTERVIEW_PREP` (behavioral, self-intro, HR/salary). The round
> where senior offers are lost by people who thought the technical rounds were the whole
> assessment. Bullets, never scripts.

---

## The STAR ratio (invert it)

| Part | Time | |
|------|:----:|---|
| Situation | 15% | just enough context |
| Task | 10% | what was yours |
| **Action** | **50%** | what *you* specifically did |
| **Result** | **25%** | what changed + what you learned |

Two rules: **separate "I" vs "we"** (they can't credit what they can't attribute), and **end every story with a lesson** (no reflection = self-promotion).

---

## The self-intro (30 sec, memorize the shape)

> "SDET, 3.8+ years at NielsenIQ across manual and automation testing. Current track:
> Playwright + TypeScript UI E2E automation with API-assisted setup/cleanup, fixtures,
> AI-assisted QA workflows, and CI execution. Earlier: Selenium + Java with RestAssured,
> SQL validation, and Jenkins. ISTQB CTFL and Azure AZ-900 certified. Looking for an SDET
> role with more ownership of automation strategy and quality engineering."

Present-first. Playwright leads. One metric. End on why. **Never** narrate from college. See `07/01`.

## The 6 stories (one-line triggers → the full note is `07/07`)

| # | Story | Your material | The lesson |
|---|-------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | Technical problem solved | Flakiness: data collisions → own-data fixtures → **under 2%** first-attempt | Don't fix before you can reproduce |
| 2 | Disagreement | API vs UI test — asked what drove their view, combined outcome | Ask what's *behind* a position first |
| 3 | Failure / were wrong | Abstracted too early, wrong page-object boundaries, refactored | Wait for the 3rd repetition |
| 4 | Influence without authority | Got devs to add `data-testid` — framed as pipeline health | The pattern was easy; the conversation was the work |
| 5 | Mentoring | Paired a new joiner on a real test day one | Fast onboarding = feedback on my design too |
| 6 | Why leaving | More architecture/strategy ownership than current scope allows | (forward-looking, no complaints) |

**Land combined outcomes ("both concerns covered"), not "I won."**

## The two-track through-line (your senior signal)

> "I've built automation in two different paradigms, so I understand where each actually
> fits — which is why I'd tell a Java team with a working Selenium grid not to migrate."

This one sentence justifies the senior title. Use it.

---

## HR / salary (full note `07/03`)

- **Salary:** *"Based on the scope and my experience across both stacks, I'm looking at 20 to 24."* Anchor on scope, no apology, **then stop talking.**
- **Current CTC (13.1):** state it honestly if asked, immediately reframe as below-market → *the reason you're leaving*. Never inflate — BGV checks it.
- **Why leaving:** forward-looking, never criticize NielsenIQ.
- **Notice:** know your actual number; buyout is a chip.
- **Pivot story (civil eng → PG-DAC → SDET):** own it as deliberate. It's a strength.
- **Scope:** keep the discussion professional, SDET-focused, and resume-backed.

## Trap questions → the shape of the answer

| Question | Answer shape |
|----------|-------------|
| "Biggest weakness?" | Real one + what you're doing about it (e.g. defaulting to solving vs delegating) |
| "5 years?" | Direction (architecture/strategy across teams), not "I'll leave in a year" |
| "Failure?" | **Never "can't think of one."** Real mistake, owned, transferable lesson |
| "Why should we pay 20+ over your 13?" | Current is the anomaly; market rate for the role is 20-24 |
| "Other offers?" | Calm, honest, never weaponize, never invent |
| "Tell me about yourself" | The 30-sec two-track. Not your CV chronologically |

---

## Prep method (per story)

1. Four bullets — S/T/A/R, not a script.
2. Say it aloud, timed → 2-3 min.
3. Cut the setup (always too long).
4. Add the "what I learned" line.
5. Prep the obvious follow-up (flakiness → "how did you know it was fixed?").

**Memorized answers sound memorized and collapse on the adjacent question.**

---

## Key takeaway

> **They can verify your technical skill in an hour — this round decides whether they want
> you in the room.** Invert the STAR ratio (half on Action), separate I/we, end every story
> with a lesson, and let the two-track through-line carry the senior title. On salary:
> anchor on scope, don't apologize, stop talking. Keep the story SDET-focused.
